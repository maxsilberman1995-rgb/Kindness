Privacy Policy

WHOOP API Compliant

Last updated: March 2025

Overview

Daily Happiness Mission is a personal wellness app that may connect to your WHOOP account to personalize daily missions using your recovery and readiness data.

Data We Collect

When you connect your WHOOP account, the App may access:

  • Recovery score and recovery state
  • Sleep performance and sleep duration
  • Daily strain score
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Respiratory rate

Journal entries, mood scores, and streak data are stored locally on your device only and never leave it.

How We Use Your Data

WHOOP data is used solely to personalize daily mission suggestions and show wellness insights. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or WHOOP health data with any third parties.

Your Rights & Revoking Access

You can disconnect your WHOOP account at any time using the Disconnect button. You can also revoke access directly in your WHOOP account under Connected Apps.

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13.
